# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers the upload tools used by the Waiver Radar and Capitol Tape channels (the Service), operated by Tony Fakhouri.

## What the Service does

The Service is a private automation that uploads videos the operator has created to the operator's own YouTube channels, and to the operator's own Instagram and TikTok accounts. It connects to those accounts only through the platforms' official APIs, using authorization granted by the account owner.

## Information we access

The Service accesses only the account owner's own channel or account, solely to upload videos and read basic upload status. It does not collect information about viewers, subscribers or any other users.

## Storage of credentials

API credentials and tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private code repository and are used only to perform the uploads described above. They are never shared or sold.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with the platform being uploaded to: Google and YouTube, Meta and Instagram, or TikTok.

## Google API Services

The Service's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy

## Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page, Instagram's Apps and Websites settings, or TikTok's app settings.
